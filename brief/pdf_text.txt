detected in this dataset, and bounded.

The biological positive control (Q2) was itself null and the closed-loop control was not available.
(d) Would we have seen it? Detection of synthetic effects added to permuted-label data
Synthetic effects of known size were added to development-split data with permuted labels before the held-out run; the
pipeline found a Q1 effect of about 0.036 (same in every experiment) or 0.063 (scaled to firing) 80% of the time, and
a Q2 growth of about 0.013 or 0.050. The earlier analysis's bound (0.06 SD, about 0.017 on this scale) is narrower
than our interval because it treats trials as independent, while ours accounts for experiments and cultures
(Supplemental Methods S1.2); the two numbers answer different questions.
